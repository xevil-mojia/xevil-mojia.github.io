Dear Hasti,

Some people walk into your life quietly and then suddenly every ordinary day has a melody in it. That is what you are to me — the soft light in a loud world.

On September 6 the universe did something generous and made you, and the rest of us have been luckier ever since. You carry that date in you always: a small, glowing signal that says kindness looks exactly like this.

I hope this new year of yours is gentle with you. I hope it hands you every small happiness you never thought to ask for — the long talks, the ridiculous laughs, the plans that finally work out, and the calm evenings you deserve a thousand of.

These photos and little moments are yours now, kept here in the dark like stars. Happy birthday, Hasti. Thank you for existing — the world got kinder the day you arrived.
