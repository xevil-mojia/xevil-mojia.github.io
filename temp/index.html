Ayda,

There are people who arrive in a room and make it louder, and then there are people like you — who walk in and somehow make everything gentler, slower, worth paying attention to.

You have this way of listening that makes a person feel like the most interesting thing in the world. You remember the small things: the song someone mentioned once, the bad week behind the smile, how they take their tea. That kind of attention is rare, and it is not small at all.

On December 29 the world got one more person who chooses kindness on purpose. I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope the things you have been quietly hoping for start arriving — all at once, in the wrong order, exactly when you need them.

Happy birthday. Thank you for existing: loudly when you want to be, softly when it matters.

— MoJia
